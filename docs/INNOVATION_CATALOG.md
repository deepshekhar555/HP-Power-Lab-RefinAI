# HPCL Innovation Catalog — 22 Ideas Across 6 Sectors

Exploratory menu of potential petroleum and energy ideas. These are not confirmed HPCL
problem statements. Verify the selected business track, challenge context, and company
priorities against the official HPCL track video before using an idea in a submission.

Every idea below is buildable with CSE + AI/ML + IoT + digital-twin skills.

---

## Sector 1 — Refining & Operations (Technological Innovation)

### 1. RefinAI — Energy and Asset-Health Decision Twin (Selected Prototype)
- **Opportunity hypothesis:** test whether unit energy and equipment-health signals can be
  brought together to help engineers prioritize reviews. Validate the actual workflow gap
  with HPCL; no refinery loss or monitoring gap is assumed here.
- **Concept:** compare approved historian data with regime-aware baselines, surface
  explainable deviations, and route review prompts to engineers. No control-system writes.
- **Prototype today:** illustrative 3D refinery scene and generated telemetry, plus public
  NASA aircraft-engine and UCI power-plant benchmark experiments. These are not HPCL data.
- **Potential impact:** measure any energy, reliability, or emissions outcome in an approved
  site pilot; no savings estimate is claimed before that evaluation.

### 2. Predictive Maintenance Twin
- Predict equipment failure (pumps, compressors, heat exchangers) before breakdown.
- Tech: vibration + temperature time-series, LSTM / survival models.
- Impact: fewer unplanned shutdowns, lower maintenance cost.

### 3. AI Yield Optimizer
- Maximize petrol/diesel yield per barrel of crude using ML process optimization.
- Tech: gradient boosting on unit operating parameters, constrained optimization.
- Impact: more saleable product from the same crude.

### 4. Emission Monitor AI
- Real-time CO2 / SO2 / NOx tracking + automated ESG & Net-Zero reporting.
- Tech: sensor fusion, forecasting, regulatory dashboards.
- Impact: compliance, carbon transparency.

### 5. Digital Thread / Process Twin
- Full virtual replica of the refinery for "what-if" simulation and operator training.
- Tech: simulation + ML surrogate models.
- Impact: safer experimentation, faster optimization.

## Sector 2 — Pipelines & Logistics

### 6. Pipeline Leak Detection AI  ⭐ TOP-3
- Detect leaks / spills early from pressure + acoustic + flow sensors.
- Tech: anomaly detection on transient pressure waves, acoustic ML classifiers.
- Impact: prevents oil spills, environmental + safety + cost win. Strong story.

### 7. Tanker Fleet Route Optimizer  ⭐ (reuses existing traffic digital-twin code)
- Smart routing for fuel tanker delivery.
- Tech: traffic digital twin + XGBoost ETA + route optimization (your existing skill).
- Impact: lower fuel cost, on-time delivery.

### 8. Fuel Supply-Demand Forecasting
- Predict how much fuel each pump needs to avoid stockout / overstock.
- Tech: time-series forecasting (seasonality, festivals, weather).
- Impact: optimized inventory, fewer dry pumps.

### 9. Drone + AI Pipeline Inspection
- Drone imagery detects corrosion, encroachment, leaks along pipelines.
- Tech: computer vision (YOLO) on aerial imagery.
- Impact: safer, cheaper, faster inspection of remote lines.

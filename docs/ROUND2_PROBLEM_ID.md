# Round 2 | Problem Identification (Two-Slide Draft)

**Proposed business track:** Technological Innovation. Confirm the exact track label and problem context against the HPCL track video before submission; the public event page does not expose that detailed brief.

## Slide 1 | Opportunity: Turn Plant Data into Earlier, Clearer Reviews

**Problem question:** How might HPCL help refinery teams spot unusual energy behavior and emerging asset-health concerns early enough to prioritize an informed operator review?

- Refinery decisions span process conditions, utility use, and equipment condition. Reviewing those signals together is a practical opportunity for better visibility and prioritization.
- The prototype does not assume HPCL lacks existing monitoring, nor does it claim a measured HPCL loss. The specific workflow gap and available tags must be validated with the chosen track video and plant stakeholders.
- Late discovery of a real efficiency or reliability issue can affect cost, availability, and emissions. The scale of any opportunity needs to be established from site data, not assumed in this proposal.

**Why HPCL / why this track:** a focused, data-led decision-support pilot could test whether existing historian signals can support more actionable unit-level reviews without replacing operator judgment or control systems.

## Slide 2 | RefinAI: A Human-Reviewed Unit Decision Twin

**Concept:** join a unit's operating context, efficiency baseline, and equipment-health indicators in one view.

1. Compare time-aligned, approved historian data with unit- and regime-specific baselines.
2. Surface deviations with the evidence, uncertainty, and relevant trend window.
3. Present maintenance or efficiency review prompts to an engineer; do not write to control systems.
4. Track accepted/rejected prompts and measure outcomes in shadow mode before considering deployment.

**What exists now:** a browser-based illustrative 3D refinery scene, simulated two-second telemetry, and reproducible public-dataset experiments for aircraft-engine RUL and combined-cycle power output.

**What remains to prove:** relevance to the selected HPCL problem, data access and quality, refinery-specific model accuracy, operator usefulness, and savings. Public benchmark results are method demonstrations, not HPCL results.
